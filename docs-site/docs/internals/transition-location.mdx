## What it is

The reporting helper returns transition x/c for each solved surface. Its inputs are the solved surface rows, transition arc length, the one-based XFOIL transition station ITRAN and the zero-based final surface index IBLTE. A transition in the wake is reported at 1.0 chord; a valid surface transition is projected onto the airfoil chord. This changes reporting, not transition physics or the solver iteration.

## Theory and index frames

The final valid surface station is IBLTE + 1 in the one-based transition frame. Therefore a transition is on the surface when:

$$
I_{\mathrm{TRAN}}\le I_{\mathrm{BLTE}}+1\label{eq:transition-location-1}
$$

Equation $\eqref{eq:transition-location-1}$ defines this relation.

Interpolate the saved surface coordinates at transition arc length, then project onto the chord vector c = TE − LE:

$$
x_{\mathrm{tr}}/c=\frac{(\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{tr}}-\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{LE}})\cdot\mathbf{c}}{\mathbf{c}\cdot\mathbf{c}}\label{eq:transition-location-2}
$$

Equation $\eqref{eq:transition-location-2}$ defines this relation.

This follows the station conventions retained from XFOIL and the existing transition projection. The previous comparison used the two index frames directly and suppressed valid near-trailing-edge transitions. The full reference case NACA0012/Re100000/Mach0.25/alpha0 exposed it: the reference reports about 0.9806 chord, while the old guard returned 1.0.

## Why and limits

Preserve the core's one-based transition convention and the array's zero-based storage convention. Converting the comparison is smaller and safer than renumbering marching, assembly and stored rows. Existing invalid arc-length/chord guards and chord-range clipping remain in place. This helper does not infer a laminar separation bubble, certify convergence or predict post-stall validity. Dedicated cases are needed for other geometries and transition settings; the required gate covers natural and forced transition on its saved matrix.
